I'm relocating from San Diego to Seattle and need temporary housing while my home sells. I'm looking for a furnished house with a yard on a week-to-week basis (timeline depends on how quickly the sale closes).

I have 2 Pomeranians and a senior cat — all well-behaved, house-trained, and up to date on vaccinations. The Poms are small and quiet, and the cat is elderly and mostly sleeps. I'm happy to provide a pet deposit or additional pet fee.

I'm looking in the 92119 / La Mesa area to stay close to my current home during the sale process.

A few things about me:

  • I'm a responsible renter — the house will be left clean
  • Flexible on move-in date

Is your property available for week-to-week rentals, and would you consider my pets? I'm happy to answer any questions.
