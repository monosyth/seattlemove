💡 Stitch Guide

Reference guide for common crochet stitches

🔗

Chain ch

The foundation stitch. Yarn over, pull through loop. Creates the base for most projects.

•

Slip Stitch sl st

Insert hook, yarn over, pull through both loops. Used for joining and moving yarn.

─

Single Crochet sc

Insert hook, yarn over, pull through, yarn over, pull through both loops. Creates tight, dense fabric.

╪

Half Double Crochet hdc

Yarn over, insert hook, yarn over, pull through, yarn over, pull through all 3 loops.

╫

Double Crochet dc

Yarn over, insert hook, yarn over, pull through, (yarn over, pull through 2) twice. Taller stitch, works up quickly.

╬

Treble Crochet tr

Yarn over twice, insert hook, yarn over, pull through, (yarn over, pull through 2) three times.

✨ Tips & Tricks

💡 Tension Tip

Consistent tension comes with practice! If your work is too tight, try holding the yarn more loosely. Too loose? Hold it a bit firmer. Everyone's natural tension is different.

🧶 Yarn Weight Guide

Fingering (1) → Sport (2) → DK (3) → Worsted (4) → Bulky (5) → Super Bulky (6). Always check your pattern for recommended weight!

📏 Gauge Matters

Always make a gauge swatch for wearables! A small difference in tension can mean the difference between a perfect fit and a project that's way off.

🔄 Magic Ring

For amigurumi and working in the round: the magic ring (magic circle) creates a closed center with no hole. Practice makes perfect!

📌 Stitch Markers

Use stitch markers to mark the first stitch of a round, pattern repeats, or increases. Even a piece of contrasting yarn works!
